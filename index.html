Our Story

A Slice of New York, Right Here

Authentic New York Pizza, Now in Asheville Our Chef, Ferrara, was born in Italy and raised in New York, where he learned the art of making authentic New York-style pizza and Italian pasta. With many years of experience perfecting his craft, Chef Ferrara brings the flavors, techniques, and traditions of a true New York pizzeria to Asheville. Now, you don’t have to travel to New York to enjoy the real thing. Come experience authentic New York-style pizza and pasta, made right here in Asheville! Born in Italy. Raised in New York. Now serving Asheville.

Ferrara Real NY Pizza was born from a simple obsession: recreating the iconic New York slice exactly as it should be — wide, foldable, with a crisp yet chewy crust that crackles on the bottom and melts into flavor at the tip.

We cold-ferment our dough for 48 hours, import San Marzano tomatoes from Campania, and hand-stretch every pie to order. No shortcuts. No compromises.

Whether you're grabbing a quick slice at the counter or settling in with a whole pie, every bite carries that unmistakable New York soul.
